Privacy Policy

MyKitBook: Traveler's Diary · Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the MyKitBook: Traveler's Diary mobile application (the “Application”) for iOS devices. It describes how information is handled when you use the Application. By using the Application, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information Collection and Use

The Application does not require registration and does not collect, store or transmit any personally identifiable information to the developer or to third parties. All content you create in the Application — including diary entries, photos, places, routes, profile name and passcode — is stored locally on your device only.

Location Information

The Application uses your device's location to attach places to diary entries, show your position on the map and, if you turn on Trip Tracker, record your route in the background. Location data is stored only on your device and is not shared with the developer. You can change location access at any time in your device settings.

Camera, Photos and Contacts

With your permission, the Application accesses the camera (to take photos and scan boarding passes), the photo library (to attach and save photos) and contacts (to choose people for your Trusted circle). This data is processed on your device and is not uploaded anywhere.

Third-Party Services

The Application uses Apple services (MapKit and Core Location) to display maps and look up place names. When these features are used, your device may send coordinates or search queries to Apple. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy. The Application does not contain advertising and does not use third-party analytics or tracking.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual entries or clear all data in the Application, or remove all data by deleting the Application from your device.

Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed to children under the age of 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Your diary can be protected with a passcode and Face ID / Touch ID. The passcode is stored in the iOS Keychain. Since your data is stored on your device, its security also depends on the security of your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. You are advised to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at michaelclode8@gmail.com.
